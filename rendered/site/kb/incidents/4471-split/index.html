5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser read dates month first

Our tenants write the day first, and the shared parser read every date month first. Day and month swapped: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” Every tenant on file had it. Fixed by engineering: the parser now reads dd/mm for these locales.

hint: booking-v3 told people to type the month first

The booking-v3 form printed MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People typed what the hint said, month first, and the form read the date the tenant’s way, day first. So the form itself swapped day and month. Only booking-v3 tenants, and only bookings made through the form. The parser fix did not touch this code. Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new reports should come from it.

one-day move: the date moves by one day, not by a month

Day and month do not swap here. The booking moves by one day: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” A client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto got different days for the same meeting. This points at time-zone handling, not at the parser. The desk already read these reports differently (desk-notes.json), but they stayed filed under parser. Open: find where a booking’s date crosses a time zone (invite, calendar, reminder).

Why one ticket hid three

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line. People write the same subjects (“Month and day swapped?”, “Wrong day on the invite”) for different bugs, so the subject cannot tell the bugs apart.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: its locale and its booking form. The “tagged as” column above shows how often the two disagree.
  • The ticket export repeats some reports across pages. The table counts each report once, by id.

What to do now

  • Engineering: take issue 3. It is the only one of the three still open.
  • Desk: merge a new report by its cause (tenant and the way the booking was made), not by its subject. A report where the date moves by one day goes to issue 3.
  • Decision 0001: it is accepted, so nobody edits it. A new decision record should replace it with merging by cause.
