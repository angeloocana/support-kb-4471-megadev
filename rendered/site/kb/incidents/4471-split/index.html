parser: the shared parser reads dates month first

Our tenants write the day first, and the shared parser reads every date month first. Day and month swap: “Booked for 2 December. The invite says 12 February.” Every tenant on file has it. Fix: parse dates in the tenant’s own locale. This is the fix #4471 queued.

hint: booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what the hint says, month first, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. So the form itself swaps day and month. Only booking-v3 tenants, and only bookings made through the form. The parser fix does not touch this code. Fix: print the tenant’s own date order under the field.

one-day move: the date moves by one day, not by a month

Day and month do not swap here. The booking moves by one day: “Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday.” A client in Lisbon and a client in Toronto got different days for the same meeting. This points at time-zone handling, not at the parser. The desk already read these reports differently (desk-notes.json), but they stayed filed under parser. Fix: find where the booking’s date crosses a time zone. The parser fix will not change it.

Why one ticket hid three

  • Decision 0001 merges on the subject line. People write the same subjects (“Month and day swapped?”, “Wrong day on the invite”) for different bugs, so the subject cannot tell the bugs apart.
  • Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. The cause depends on the tenant: its locale and its booking form. The “tagged as” column below shows how often the two disagree.
  • The ticket export repeats some reports across pages. Each report is counted once, by id.
